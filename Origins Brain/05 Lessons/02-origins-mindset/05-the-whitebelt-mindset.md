## Slide notes

Why you should never act like you know everything. s/o GSP.

Georges St-Pierre: “I keep the white belt mentality that I can learn from anyone, anywhere, anytime.”

If you act like you know everything, you can’t learn → can’t grow. Someone doing $100/day can still teach someone at $100k/day.

Two rooms: flexing “I’m at 10k heading to 50k, best at Facebook ads” vs listening even when you’re the bigger player. Spencer: Dubai talk, four guys in the back taking notes who do ~$10M/month — they still picked things up. A health/wellness CEO (unnamed, later in Evolve) asked him what was new as a student.

Don’t be the loudest. Always listen. When you stop learning you stop growing; if you’re not growing you’re dying.
