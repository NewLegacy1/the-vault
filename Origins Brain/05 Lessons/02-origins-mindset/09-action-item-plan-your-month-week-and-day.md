### Time To Put Into Action

The Skool lesson body is empty on the live page (heading only; `desc` and `resources` in the API are also empty). The actual plan lives in the previous module.

Do this using [How to plan to get to $1,000+ daily](08-how-to-plan-to-get-to-1000-daily.md):

1. Write **one monthly goal per bucket** (Ads + CRO). Directional, measurable, a stretch you can still hit.
2. For this week, write **1–2 outcomes per bucket** that must be true by Friday for the month to stay on track.
3. For tomorrow, pull **1–2 tasks** from the week, time-block them, and plan the night before.
4. Keep goals as **inputs you control** (batches shipped, offers launched), not “an ad must win.”

Example from that lesson: $100/day → $300/day in 30 days.

- Ads: launch 3–5 ad batches per week (goal: find at least 1 winner this month)
- CRO: launch a “Buy 2, Save 15%” bundle

Winner language from that lesson:

- Spend winners: 30–50%+ of spend, but you still cannot raise budget
- Breakthroughs: 30–50%+ of spend **and** you can raise daily budget at target ROAS
