## Slide notes — cycle of change

You joined because you want to become someone who earns more / has more skill. Minds reject change (iPhone headphone-jack analogy).

Place yourself on the graph:

1. **Uninformed optimism** — excited, screenshots of $100k days, “I can do this.”
2. **Informed pessimism** — Shopify, product, creatives, mediabuying… not as easy as it looked.
3. **Valley of despair** — “this is stupid, it’ll never work.” **This is where unsuccessful people quit** and hop to TikTok Shop / real estate / the next thing, repeating the same loop.
4. **Shortcut luck** — copy-paste Kalodata / ripped ads works briefly, then you slide back to the valley because you skipped real growth.
5. **The climb (3 → 4)** — “the work works more on you than you work on it” (Hormozi). Feedback loops. This is where sustainable success is made.
6. **Informed optimism / success** — conviction, it starts working.

Time horizon is not 30–60 days. Consistency without feedback loops is not enough (next video). You don’t currently have the skillset for the next level. Copy-paste magic wouldn’t last anyway. Goal is **100k/month every month**, which requires becoming a different person.
