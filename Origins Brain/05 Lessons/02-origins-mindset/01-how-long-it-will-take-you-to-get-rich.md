## Slide notes — 10,000-hour math

World-class via deliberate practice (~10,000 hours). $100k/month might be ~$20–30k profit; you’ll likely want more. Spencer’s own path: May 2020 start, serious in 2022, ~3.5 years of ~12h days → not claiming world-class but “know more than most.”

| Pace | Every day | One day off / week | Weekends off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 24h/day | ~1.14 years | n/a | n/a |
| 12h/day | ~2y 3m | ~2y 8m | ~3y 2m |
| 8h/day | ~3y 5m | ~4 years | ~4y 9m |
| 4h/day | ~6y 10m | ~8 years | ~almost a decade |

Halve it (~5,000 hours) if “good enough for $100k/month”: 12h every day ≈ 1 year. Realistic grind (8–12h, some days off) ≈ **~3 years**. Not meant to demotivate — reset 30/60-day get-rich expectations. Evolve tagline: “Get rich **quick**” with *quick* crossed out. Luck without skill doesn’t last. You don’t have to stay in Origins 3 years, but you do have to commit to the skill for years.
