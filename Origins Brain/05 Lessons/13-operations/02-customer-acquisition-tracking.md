## Slide / lesson notes

Learn how to track the metrics that drive profitable growth. By monitoring customer acquisition, marketing efficiency, and customer behaviour, you'll understand whether your advertising is working, when it's safe to increase spend, and which metrics deserve your attention every day.

### 💡 Key Takeaways & Highlights:

🎯 Track New Customers, Returning Customers, CAC, NROAS
- 
📉 Understand if your marketing is becoming more or less efficient
- 
📊 Measure the quality of your customer acquisition over time
- 
🚀 Learn when to scale advertising and when to optimise performance
- 
📈 Build a daily growth dashboard to support better decision-making
