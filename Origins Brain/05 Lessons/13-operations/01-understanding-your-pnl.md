## Slide / lesson notes

Learn how to build and use a simple daily P&L dashboard to understand the financial health of your business. You'll discover how to track revenue, margins, shipping, advertising, and operating costs so you can identify where you're making money, where you're losing it, and make better decisions with confidence.

### 💡 Key Takeaways & Highlights:

📈 Understand the journey from Gross Sales → Net Sales → CM1 → CM2 → CM3
- 
💰 Learn what each metric means and why it matters
- 
🔍 Identify where profit is leaking in your business
- 
🚀 Know whether your business is healthy enough to scale
- 
📊 Build a daily financial dashboard you can review in under 5 minutes
