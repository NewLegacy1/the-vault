## Slide / lesson notes

How to extract powerful marketing insights from your existing customer base! Discover how gathering customer feedback allows you to unearth unexpected buying motivations, discover hidden sub-avatars, and develop high-converting ad angles.

### 💡 Key Takeaways & Highlights:

🔍 Uncovering Hidden Buying Motivations: Learn why customers often buy products for completely different reasons than the ones you originally targeted in your ads.
- 
🛠️ Module Roadmap: Overview of what’s covered in this module series—from setting up post-purchase surveys from scratch to automating trigger emails and incentivizing customer completion.
- 
🤖 Scaling Data Analysis with AI: How to review feedback individually as a small brand, and how to leverage Virtual Assistants (VAs) or AI analysis tools (like Claude) to process large volumes of survey responses.
- 
💡 The "Aha!" Moment: Discovering how understanding true customer purchasing triggers opens up entirely new audience segments and marketing opportunities for your business.
