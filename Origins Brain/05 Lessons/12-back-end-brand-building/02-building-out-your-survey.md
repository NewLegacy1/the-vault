## Slide / lesson notes

Learn how to build a comprehensive Post-Purchase Survey to capture invaluable feedback from recent buyers. Discover how to structure survey questions, offer compelling incentives, and gather actionable data to optimize your PDPs, fix website pain points, and discover new product ideas! PS - You can use any data collection form with a database repository, i use notion. 

### 💡 Key Takeaways & Highlights:

🛠️ Form & Database Integration: How to build surveys using platforms like Noteforms, Google Forms, or Typeform, and seamlessly sync responses directly into a Notion database.
- 
🎁 High-Converting Incentive Strategy: How to maximize response rates by offering a monthly giveaway (e.g., refunding orders for randomly selected respondents) immediately after checkout while customers are in a enthusiastic buying state.
- 
❓ Question Breakdown & Insights:
Awareness & Discovery: Identify exact channels driving traffic and customer acquisition.
- 
UX & Shopping Experience: Spot website bugs, broken buttons, or missing size charts to improve site conversion.
- 
Purchasing Drivers & Sub-Avatars: Uncover specific pain points (e.g., back pain) and target personas (e.g., buying for self vs. gifting).
- 
Handling Objections: Identify customer concerns (shipping times, trust issues, product doubts) to overcome them in future ad copy.
- 
Product Expansion Opportunities: Ask customers what products are missing to surface demand for new product SKUs.
