## Slide / lesson notes

How to locate and copy the direct URL / Post ID for your active Facebook ads directly from Meta Ads Manager! This link can be used in data-scraping tools like Apify to extract customer comments and insights.

### 💡 Step-by-Step Breakdown:

📂 Navigate Ads Manager: Open Meta Ads Manager, select your ad account, and go to your ad set.
- 
👁️ Preview the Ad: Select your desired ad and click the "Preview" button.
- 
💬 Open Facebook Post: In the preview menu, select "See Post" ➔ "Facebook Post with Comments".
- 
🔗 Copy URL: Copy the resulting Facebook post URL from your browser’s address bar (this URL contains the direct Post ID needed for comment scraping with Apify).
