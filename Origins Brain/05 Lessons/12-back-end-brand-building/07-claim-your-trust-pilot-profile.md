## Slide / lesson notes

Learn step-by-step how to locate, claim, and set up your official Trustpilot for Business account, as well as how to retrieve your direct review-submission link to collect customer feedback.
🔍 Locating Your Store: Search Trustpilot using your brand name or store URL to find your aggregated business page.
- 
🏷️ Claiming an Unclaimed Profile: Identify the "Unclaimed Profile" status banner on your page and click through to initiate ownership verification.
- 
✉️ Account Setup: Complete registration using your business email address to verify your domain and unlock your official Trustpilot for Business account.
- 
🔗 Retrieving the Review Link: Navigate to the blue "Write a Review" button on your profile page to generate and copy the direct evaluation URL (/evaluate/your-brand-name) for future customer review requests.
