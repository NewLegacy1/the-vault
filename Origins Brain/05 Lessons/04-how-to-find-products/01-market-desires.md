## Slide notes

- **What is Desire?** Desire is the force that makes advertising work. You cannot create it; it already exists in the market (wants, needs, wishes).
- **Permanent vs Trending Desire**
  - Permanent: health, wealth, relationships (never leave; most ads map back to these).
  - Trending: crypto, Valentine’s Day, AI (come and go; useful for short-term spikes).
- **Desire Strength:** intensity (minor headache vs migraine) × scope (how many people share it). Stronger desire = more scalable ads.
- **Airplane metaphor:** your ad is the plane; desire is a tailwind. Weak/no desire is a headwind (or the ad goes nowhere).
- **Fitness example (increasing strength):**
  1. Toned calves (weak / niche)
  2. Gain more muscle
  3. Get a six pack
  4. Have a healthy physique (strongest / mass)
- **Physical vs functional product:** people buy the outcome (e.g. celebrity-style hair), not the motherboard. Physical specs justify quality; function drives the purchase.
- **Resource recommended in-video:** *Breakthrough Advertising* (Eugene Schwartz) — instructor said it 3x’d his business; no affiliation.
