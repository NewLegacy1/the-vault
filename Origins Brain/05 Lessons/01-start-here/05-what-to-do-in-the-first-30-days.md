## Key points / checklist (slides)

After mindset + friend + product + avatars, 30-day ROI path:

1. **Create ads like a marketer** — not “hack Facebook” or “find a magic product.” Goal: **one concept per day** (5 days/week minimum). Follow ad definitions, angles, etc.
2. **Find one strong converting offer** — compact module; one offer that isn’t the bottleneck.
3. **Complete feedback loops on every ad** — review your concepts + study other winning ads (Winning Ad Deck). Early on it’s “monkey see, monkey do.”
4. **Meta BM + ad account set up properly.**
5. **Spend 90% of time outside Ads Manager** — make the account look good by working on ads/offers/learnings, not staring at spend.

Winning ad (for most people here) = profitable, or lets you scale (e.g. $10k→$15k/month or $1k→$2k/day). Finding one in 30 days is possible but not the real bar; knowing and doing the loop is the W. ROI bar: **one ad that makes ~$100/month profit** pays for Origins.
