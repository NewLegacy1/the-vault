## Key points

Two things in two days:

1. **Fix mindset / break limiting beliefs.** Watch the Origins Mindset modules (especially the first three, plus “why you never fail” and “why hard is good”). Spencer says they still do this with ~$10M/month brand owners. Without this, the rest of the program “won’t work.”
2. **Find one person in the community (ideally local).** Use the Skool **Map** tab. Don’t ditch old friends dramatically — be cordial — but lock in with people who get ecom. Meet in person only after talking (Zoom first). Even one or two connections matters more than an “accountability buddy” label.

First W in the program = those two actions.
