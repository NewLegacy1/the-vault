## Key points / slides

Checkpoint for week 1:

1. **Double down or find your product.** Optional: watch the separate “0–$100k/month” classroom webinar (~1 hour, shows 2 hours) for 0–10k / 10–50k / 50–100k checkpoints. Any product can scale if you learn marketing; some are easier. Use Sean’s winning-products + Kalodata lessons. If it takes a month or two, that’s OK — skillset > first-week ROI.
2. **Do research and build avatars.** “How To Prepare For Ads” / avatar training is called the densest and most valuable beginner section. Spencer says he didn’t learn proper research for ~2.5 years and avatars the right way for ~4 years.

Deadlines are there to create school-style urgency. Finishing that folder is a W because it’s the same info used by brands going to $100k/day.
