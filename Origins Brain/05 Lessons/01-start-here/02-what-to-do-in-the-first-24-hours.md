## Key points

- First action is a **value post**, not a “hi I’m Spencer” intro. Idea taken from Alex Hormozi’s ACQ Skool group.
- People value people who give value (reciprocity). Questions-only posts make you look needy.
- Does **not** have to be about your brand, ads, or revenue. Personal habits, tools, or brands you study are enough.
- Post in the **🍝 Value** category.
- Template vibe: who you are + where you’re at + one useful thing (e.g. Opal.so for focus / not living in Ads Manager).
- If you can’t think of ecom sauce, share something from life (golf, boxing, etc.).
- After this, the 48-hour video covers Map + connecting with people.
