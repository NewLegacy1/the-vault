# Learnings Loop

Close the loop so the next ad is smarter.

- Study **winners** (yours or others) to correct your eye.
- Study **losers that got spend** — Meta pushed them; something hooked.
- Pull frameworks: hook, offer, mechanism, story — not “nice creative.”
- Keep the piece that got signal (e.g. a headline inside a losing batch).
- Write learnings into the Creative Roadmap **before** marking a concept Done.
- Weekly: break ads into avatar, feeling, awareness, copy theory.

Cheat sheet before briefing video: first 1–2s hook the *right* user; native to feed; clear mental image; who it’s for; authentic; applied past winners; educates toward core desire; separates you; claim escalates.
