# Back End Brand

After you have customers, mine **why they bought** (often not what the ad said). That’s new angles.

- Post-purchase survey + giveaway incentive.
- Apify comments from a **real post URL**, not Ads Library. Read comments yourself.
- Trustpilot: 4–5 stars public; 1–3 stars private (Judge.me/support). Trigger on **delivered**.

You need customers first. Small brand: read surveys yourself.
