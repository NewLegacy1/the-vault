# Meta Assets

- Create the **Page on the personal profile**, not inside Business Manager. If BM dies, the page remains.
- Ad account timezone = **where you sell**.
- Warm-up: $5/day page-like ads → about **50 approved ads** → then sales.
- Pixel: default **Shopify Meta** integration, max matching. Not Wetracked first.
- Put **3+ real profiles** as BM admins so one ban doesn’t wipe access.
- Verify BM is optional.
