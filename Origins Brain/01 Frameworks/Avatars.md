# Avatars

Core avatars should be **desire-based** (May 2026 update), not demographic-first.

- Desires create strong avatars. Hunt them; don’t invent them.
- **Sub-avatars** niche down (life stage, context, behaviors, feelings) until you find an underserved group.
- Broader avatar = offer, proof, and creative all have to work harder.
- Use a **desire calendar** (seasons + holidays). Brands that die in January often ignored what people want that month.

Fill the product understanding doc **before** desires/avatars. You can’t sell what you don’t understand.
