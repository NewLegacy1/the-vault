# Market Sophistication

How many similar products/claims the market has already seen (Schwartz). Stage decides **what your ad is allowed to say**.

| Stage | Market has heard | You do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Nothing | Name the claim |
| 2 | The claim | Enlarge the claim |
| 3 | Enlarged claims | New **mechanism** |
| 4 | Mechanisms | Enlarge the mechanism |
| 5 | Everything | **Identity** (Nike/Rolex). Most Origins students skip for now. |

Cycle can reset with a new mechanism or an underserved [[Avatars|avatar]].

**If you are not first to market, you win with a new mechanism or an underserved avatar** — not by spam-testing random AliExpress SKUs.

Be ethical with “new information.” Double-check AI. Don’t fabricate claims.
