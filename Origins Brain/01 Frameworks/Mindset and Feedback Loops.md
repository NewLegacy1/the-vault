# Mindset and Feedback Loops

- Cycle: uninformed optimism → informed pessimism → **valley of despair** (where most quit) → climb via feedback loops.
- Shortcut luck (ripped ads) slides back.
- Realistic grind to real skill ≈ years, not 30-day get-rich. $100k/month every month means becoming a different person.
- White belt: learn from anyone. Ego blocks growth.
- Failure is data. Hard is the filter that creates value.
- After a product is live: two buckets — **Ads** and **CRO**.
- Plan month → week → day around **inputs you control** (concepts shipped, offers launched).
- **Spender:** 30–50%+ of spend, cannot raise budget. **Breakthrough:** 30–50%+ of spend **and** you can raise daily budget at target ROAS. You want breakthroughs.
