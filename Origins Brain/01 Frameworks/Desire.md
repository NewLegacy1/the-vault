# Desire

You **cannot create desire**. It already exists (wants, needs, wishes). Ads channel it; they do not manufacture it.

## Permanent vs trending

- **Permanent:** health, wealth, relationships. Never leave. Most winning ads map back here.
- **Trending:** crypto, holidays, AI. Come and go. Useful for spikes, not the long-term engine.

## Strength

Strength = **intensity** (headache vs migraine) × **scope** (how many people). Stronger desire = more scalable ads.

Airplane: your ad is the plane; desire is tailwind. Weak desire is headwind.

## Physical vs functional

People buy the **outcome** (celebrity hair), not the motherboard. Specs justify quality; function drives purchase.

## Fitness ladder (weak → strong)

Toned calves → more muscle → six pack → healthy physique.

## Research cue

Look for “I was looking for…” / “I needed…” language. Then **test desires in ads**. Same product, different desire = different scale.

Recommended book in-course: *Breakthrough Advertising* (Eugene Schwartz).
