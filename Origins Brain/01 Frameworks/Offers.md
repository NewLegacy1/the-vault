# Offers

Offers + ads are the fastest scale levers. A mediocre page makes already-hard ads harder.

- Recalc **breakeven ROAS after every offer**. Offers can 2× ROAS overnight and also nuke margin.
- Margin: try **>60%**. Floor ~50%. Sweet spot 60–70%.
- Types: **bundle & save** (default), conditional, BOGO / % off second, free gifts. Name the bundle; give it a reason.
- Offer ≠ price. Value = likelihood of result + time + less sacrifice + risk removal.
- Weak FAQs, shipping, reviews, photos = weak offer even at a “deal” price.

Build → duplicate winning ads to the new page → test 3–7 days in the 1-campaign setup.
