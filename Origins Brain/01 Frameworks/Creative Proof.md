# Creative Proof

Before you pick a product, confirm people **already spend** to sell something like it — and that you still have a lane.

Look at:

- Meta Ads Library
- Pipiads / Adspy (via [[Tools Stack]])
- Foreplay for static patterns

Ask:

- What **mechanism** or **avatar** are they using?
- Is this stage 3–4 sophistication (claims already enlarged)?
- Can I do **new mechanism** or **underserved avatar** instead of a copycat?

Ripped creatives are borrowed time. You still have to make your own ads.
