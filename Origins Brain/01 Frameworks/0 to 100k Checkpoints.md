# 0 to 100k Checkpoints

From the live webinar:

1. **$0–$10k/mo:** one offer people actually want (not just a cheaper price). Rebuild the offer before blaming the product.
2. **$10k–$50k/mo:** one winning ad + underserved avatar. One great creator beats an agency.
3. **$50k–$100k/mo:** one optimized customer journey after the click (CRO).

Don’t hunt a secret product. Hunt **one offer, one ad, one journey**. Almost any product can hit $100k/month with good marketing.
