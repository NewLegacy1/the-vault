# Unit Economics

- Beginner retail band: about **$40–$80** (not $100+ first).
- **3× (COGS + shipping)** should still sit in the expected market price.
- Kalodata filter (product research): last 7 days, avg unit price >$15, sales growing, skip mega brands, USPTO check.
- P&L path: Gross → Net → **CM1** (after COGS) → **CM2** (after ship/fulfill) → **CM3** (after marketing). Scale from CM3, not vanity ROAS.
- Acquisition: CAC, NROAS, % new vs return. Two good days → scale ~20%. Bad CAC → cut.

Confirm a [[Tools Stack|Matedropshipping]] quote **before** you commit to a product.
